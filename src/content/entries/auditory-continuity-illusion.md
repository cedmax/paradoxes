problem: "Can a tone seem to carry on through a burst of noise that replaced it?"
why: "When a louder sound covers the place where a quieter one stops, hearing can supply the missing stretch, so the tone seems to continue behind the noise. Here no tone is played during the burst. Warren and Obusek found this works when the louder sound would have masked the quieter one had both been present, and that the level difference needed varies widely with the sounds used. With a quiet burst the gap is heard as a gap. How the brain fills the gap is described in more than one way."
