problem: "Can reason establish both that the world began and that it never began?"

setup: "Kant presented arguments that time must have a beginning and arguments that time cannot have a beginning. Both look persuasive when the world is treated as one complete thing."
whyWrong: "The first argument says an endless past could never be completed. The second says a beginning seems to need a time before it. They pull towards opposite answers to one question."
resolution:
  - "Kant took the clash to show that reason was applying ideas suited to experience beyond their proper limits. For the first antinomy, he argued that neither side can simply be asserted about the world as a thing in itself."
  - "Others reject Kant’s framework and try to assess the rival premises or develop accounts of time and infinity that do settle the question. The dispute is about the reach of the reasoning, not whether Kant wrote down two arguments."
