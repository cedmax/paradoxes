problem: "Can two beeps make one flash look like two?"
why: "A single brief flash can seem to happen twice when two nearby beeps play around it. The sound changes some viewers' report of what they saw even though the visual stimulus never changes. The effect depends on timing, display and listener; some people still see just one flash."
finePrint: "Shams, Kamitani and Shimojo studied this illusion in 2002 and found an audio-visual interaction window of roughly 100 milliseconds. This demonstration aims for a single 30 ms visible colour change, with 14 ms beeps centred 45 ms before and after its onset (or one beep centred on it). These timings and the 750 Hz tone are chosen for this site. The flash is aligned to estimated audio output timing and display frames, but audio-device latency, frame refresh and Bluetooth delays can still introduce error. This is not a calibrated experiment."
