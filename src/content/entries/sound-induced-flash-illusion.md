problem: "Can two beeps make one flash look like two?"
why: "A single brief flash can seem to happen twice when two nearby beeps play around it. The sound changes some viewers' report of what they saw even though the visual stimulus never changes. The effect depends on timing, display and listener; some people still see just one flash."
finePrint: "Shams, Kamitani and Shimojo reported this illusion in 2000 and experimentally examined it in 2002. The second study found an audio-visual interaction window of roughly 100 milliseconds. This demonstration uses a single 30 ms change of a disc's colour, with one or two short beeps centred 45 ms before and after its onset. Those parameters are chosen for this site. Browser sound scheduling and the display's refresh rate can change the effective timing, so this is not a calibrated experiment."
