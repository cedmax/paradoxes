why: "The arrows are fixed at right angles on opposite faces of the coin, but flipping it about a vertical axis reverses the horizontal direction in the view. After a 45° clockwise turn, the front arrow points up-right and the arrow in the flipped view points down-left. After an anticlockwise turn, they both appear to point up-left. The puzzle is keeping track of the turn and the flip together."
