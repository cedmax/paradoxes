problem: "Can the same repeating beeps sound like one galloping rhythm or like two separate streams?"
why: "Hearing tends to group sounds that are close in pitch and timing and to separate those that are not, which helps us follow one voice among others. In this pattern a small pitch gap lets the notes join into a single galloping rhythm, while a larger gap or a faster pace tends to split them into a high stream and a low one. In between, listeners can hear either, and what they hear can switch. The notes and their timing never change, only how the sounds are grouped. The exact boundaries depend on the speed, the listener and where attention is."
